# Official source starting points

Recheck links, coverage, access terms, and publication dates at the time of research. The issuer's exchange, regulator, and investor-relations site determine the relevant primary sources. A market data vendor can supplement them for quotes and peer screens; verify timestamp, currency, share class, split adjustments, and corporate actions before using its figures.

## Baraka access

- [Baraka markets](https://getbaraka.com/markets?market=us): discover instruments; confirm current availability in the user's app/account before calling one tradable.
- [Baraka fee support](https://getbaraka.com/support/does-baraka-charge-any-fees-for-buying-or-selling-stocks-or-etfs): check the current plan, trade, conversion, and transfer costs relevant to the investor. Do not hard-code current fees into a recommendation.
- [Baraka UAE market overview](https://getbaraka.com/gcc-stocks-overview): a starting point for UAE listings; check actual account access and supported venues.

## United States and SEC-reporting issuers

- [SEC EDGAR filing search](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings): annual and interim reports, current reports, proxy statements, insider ownership reports, and amendments.
- [SEC EDGAR public data APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces): submissions and XBRL company facts. These APIs do not require a key. Check [SEC developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources) and fair-access terms before automated retrieval.
- [Investor.gov guide to using EDGAR](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/researching-investments/using-edgar-research-investments) and [guide to Form 10-K](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/researching-investments/how-read-10-k): document orientation, not substitutes for the actual filings.

## Saudi Arabia and United Arab Emirates

- [Saudi Exchange issuer announcements](https://www.saudiexchange.sa/wps/portal/saudiexchange/newsandreports/issuer-news/issuer-announcements?locale=en): financial results and material issuer disclosures. Follow the listed issuer's financial statements, annual report, and company profile from the exchange or issuer site.
- [Dubai Financial Market](https://www.dfm.ae/en) and [Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange](https://www.adx.ae/en/investors/investors-information): start with issuer disclosures for UAE-listed candidates.
- [Saudi Central Bank statistics](https://sama.gov.sa/en-US/Publications/EconomicReports/Pages/database.aspx) when rates, credit, or domestic macro conditions are material to the thesis.

## Other markets and macro data

- Find the applicable exchange and securities regulator first; use their issuer announcement or filing system and the issuer's investor-relations site. Confirm whether filings are consolidated, audited, and available in the same accounting basis and currency as peers.
- Use [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/), [World Bank indicators](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392), or [IMF data](https://data.imf.org/en/Resource-Pages/IMF-API) only for macro assumptions that materially affect the comparison; identify series, geography, observation period, and revisions.

## Investor risk context

- [Investor.gov asset allocation guidance](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation) explains the role of horizon, risk tolerance, and diversification.
- [Investor.gov fraud guidance](https://www.investor.gov/protect-your-investments/fraud/protect-your-money) warns against claims of guaranteed high returns.
